## Practices to reuse

- Start from the product's principal purpose, core tasks and outcomes. Choose what the reader needs to recognize before deciding which screenshot or diagram is convenient.
- Prefer representative actual screenshots or cases. When suitable material is unavailable, use a grounded capability/workflow illustration as described in [readme-visuals.md](../../references/readme-visuals.md).
- Use short action or capability labels. If a review area matters to the product, make it explicit rather than hiding it under a vague label such as “handle problems.” Avoid turning labels into questions or explanation paragraphs.
- Group the work into a few readable areas. Preserve necessary capabilities while reducing repeated titles, visual clutter and oversized typography. Give the content room to breathe.
- Coordinate the image with the README first screen. In this case the page already names the product, so the illustration does not repeat a large product title or slogan. Its illustrative alt text identifies the visual without an “effect preview” heading.
- Inspect the actual image and the rendered README. Show a new or changed visual to the owner and obtain approval before publication. File/path checks do not establish truth, readability, rights or approval.

## Choices specific to this case

The three groups, nine labels, folder/review/package objects, 16:9 composition, light background, blue objects, small green accents and Chinese in-image wording belong to this product and the approved artwork. They are examples, not universal requirements. A different Skill needs its own capability map, visual form, language and appropriate layout; do not copy Publisher's review categories into unrelated products.

The generated asset was reviewed for its actual visible text and layout and approved by the owner for this publication. It contains no account data, corporate logo, fabricated interface, metrics or pass certificates. Generation prompts and iteration records are kept in the local task review, not embedded in public README captions.
